## Full description (4000 char limit)

```
Anyone can tick a box. Cairn asks for proof.

Cairn is a habit tracker with one rule that changes everything: every completion needs a photo, and that photo is checked by AI before it counts. Went for the run? Show the trail. Tidied the desk? Show the desk. Read the chapter? Show the page. If the photo does not show what you claimed, it does not count.

That small friction is the whole idea. It is the difference between a streak you earned and a streak you tapped.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Set a habit. Daily, certain weekdays, or a day of the month. Cairn works out when it is due.
2. Photograph the proof. Take it now, or pick a recent shot. Old photos are rejected, so you cannot dig through last year's camera roll.
3. Get it verified. An AI checks the photo genuinely shows the habit you claimed. Pass, and you place a stone.

BUILD SOMETHING THAT STACKS UP

Every verified proof places a stone on that habit's cairn. Ten stones cap a cairn and a new one begins. Break the streak and the current cairn seals early, unfinished, exactly as it happened.

Your trail becomes an honest record. Not a wall of green squares, but a line of monuments, some complete, some cut short, each one built from something you actually did.

CLIMB

Every stone earns metres. Keep a streak and each day is worth more. Finish everything due in a day and earn a bonus. Cap a cairn and earn more still. Climb from Pebble through Ridge, Crag and Bluff, up to Peak and finally Summit at 8,849 metres.

Your rank only ever goes up. Nothing you have earned can be taken away.

WHAT ELSE

- Works offline. Prove a habit with no signal and it is recorded straight away, then verified when you reconnect. Your streak is never punished for a dropped connection.
- Free sync. Create an account and your habits, stones and rank follow you to any device. Syncing costs nothing.
- Real statistics. Consistency, current and longest streaks, cairns built, and how the climb is going.
- No ads. Not now, not later.
- No tracking. Cairn contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs at all.

PRIVACY, PLAINLY

Cairn is local-first. Your habits and your proof photos live on your device. A photo is sent for verification at the moment you submit it and is not stored on our servers afterwards. You can use the entire app without ever giving us an email address.

CAIRN PREMIUM

Cairn is free, and the free version is genuinely useful: unlimited habits, free sync, and five AI-verified proofs a day.

Cairn Premium is an optional subscription that removes the daily cap, so you can prove as many habits as you keep. It is available monthly or annually, with a 7-day free trial. Subscriptions renew automatically and can be cancelled any time in Google Play.

Some features shown on the Premium screen are marked "Coming soon". Those are planned, not finished, and are clearly labelled so you always know what you are paying for today.

Stop performing consistency. Start proving it.
```
