Cairn Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026
Cairn is a habit tracker where you prove each completed habit with a photo. It is built local-first: your habits and your photos live on your device. This policy explains the few cases where data leaves it, and what you can do about that.

Who we are

Cairn ("the app") is an independent app published by the developer listed under Contact. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean that developer, and "you" means the person using the app.

The short version

  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. The app contains none.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing.
  • You can use Cairn without giving us an email address or any other identifying detail.
  • Your proof photos are not stored on our servers.

What data Cairn handles

Data stored on your device

Your habits, schedules, completions, streaks, progress and your proof photos are stored locally on your device. If you never create an account and never complete a proof, no personal data leaves your device at all.

An automatic anonymous ID

On first launch, Cairn creates an anonymous account with our backend provider (Supabase) so the app can talk to our verification service securely. This produces a random user identifier. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number, device advertising ID, or any other identifier, and it is not used for tracking or profiling.

If you create an account (optional)

You may optionally upgrade to an account so your records sync across devices. In that case we process your email address (to send a verification code and to let you reset your password) and a securely hashed password, handled by our authentication provider. Your habits, completions and verification records then sync to our database, where access is restricted to your own account by row-level security.

Proof photos and how verification works

This is the part worth reading closely, because it is the one place your photo leaves your device.

  • You take a photo with the camera, or choose an existing one from your photo library.
  • Cairn compresses it and saves it on your device.
  • To verify it, the app sends the image, together with the title and optional description of the habit it is meant to prove, over an encrypted connection to our verification service, which forwards it to Google's Gemini API to judge whether the photo plausibly shows that habit being done.
  • Our verification service does not store the image. The image data passes through and is discarded once the check returns. We keep only the resulting verdict (whether it passed, a confidence score, and a short reason).
  • Your photos are not uploaded to our storage and are not backed up by us. Cloud photo backup is not currently offered.
  • The app reads a photo's capture time (from your photo library or the file's EXIF data) on your device only, to check the photo is recent. That timestamp is not transmitted to us.

Google processes the image as our service provider in order to return a verdict. Your use of Cairn's verification therefore also involves Google's handling of that image at the moment of the check. We do not control, and this policy does not cover, Google's own practices; see Google's privacy documentation for its API services.

Subscriptions

Cairn Premium is an optional paid subscription. Purchases are processed by Google Play, and subscription status is managed for us by RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives your anonymous Cairn user identifier and purchase and transaction information so the app knows whether your subscription is active.

We never receive or store your payment card details. Those are handled entirely by Google Play.

How long we keep things

  • Proof photos: kept on your device. Cairn deletes the local file when a proof is rejected. Everything local is removed when you uninstall the app.
  • Images sent for verification: not retained by us.
  • Account and synced records: kept while your account exists, until you ask us to delete them.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and the data associated with it, email us at the address under Contact from the email address registered to your Cairn account, with the subject "Delete my account".

We will delete your account and its synced records (habits, completions and verification records) within 30 days. Data stored only on your device is removed by uninstalling the app.

If you never created an account, there is no personal data for us to delete: the anonymous identifier is not linked to you, and deleting the app removes your local data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete personal data we hold about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority. To exercise any of these, contact us at the address below. We will not charge you or degrade your use of the app for asking.

Security

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Access to synced records is restricted per account at the database level. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the amount of data we hold deliberately small, which is the most reliable protection available.

Children

Cairn is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

International transfers

Our service providers may process and store data on servers outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards offered by those providers.
