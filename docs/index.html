Cairn

Don't just check it off.
Prove it.

A habit tracker where every completion is backed by a photo, checked by AI. No more tapping a box and lying to yourself.

How it works

Three steps, every time. The friction is the point.

1

Set a habit

Daily, weekly, or a specific day of the month. Cairn works out when it is due.

2

Photograph the proof

Snap the run, the tidy desk, the finished page. It has to be recent, so no digging through old photos.

3

Get it verified

AI checks the photo actually shows the habit you claimed. Pass, and you place a stone.

Build something that stacks up

Every verified proof places a stone. Ten stones cap a cairn, and a new one begins. Break the streak and the cairn seals early, unfinished, exactly as it happened. Your trail is an honest record of what you actually did.

Streaks that cannot be faked

A streak means proof for every scheduled day, not a tapped checkbox.

Climb through the ranks

Earn metres for every stone and rise from Pebble to Summit.

Works offline

Prove a habit with no signal. It is recorded and verified when you reconnect.

Sync is free

Add an account and your trail follows you to any device, at no cost.

Quiet by design

Cairn is built local-first, and deliberately collects very little.

  • No ads, and no analytics or tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • Your proof photos stay on your device. They are checked, not stored by us.
  • You can use the whole app without ever giving an email address.
