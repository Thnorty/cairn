Cairn Premium · billing states
Companion to Cairn Premium.dc.html: the states the paywall enters once real billing is wired. Every element reuses the existing palette (sage #6d8056, clay #c07a54, parchment #e9e1d3), Zilla Slab + Work Sans, and the app's existing button / snackbar / panel shapes. Approved 2026-07-24.
1  Trial button · purchasing
Starting your trial…
While the store sheet is up and the purchase resolves: spinner + disabled, in place. No new screen, no route change. Plan cards are non-interactive for the duration.
2  Success
You're on Cairn Premium. Climb on.
On success the paywall closes and the daily-cap gate lifts reactively (Unlimited AI proofs is active immediately). A sage confirmation snackbar, deliberately not a celebration screen: a purchase is a transaction, not a milestone. Cairn Complete stays the only celebration.
3  Purchase error
Purchase couldn't be completed. Please try again.
The app's existing dark message snackbar. A user cancel is silent: no snackbar, button returns to idle. Cancelling is not an error.
4  Restore · two outcomes
Purchases restored.
No purchases to restore.
Restore reports either outcome plainly. "Restore purchase" in the footer shows the same in-place spinner treatment as the trial button while it runs.
